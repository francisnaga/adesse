Delete your account

Template. Replace the highlighted [placeholders]. Google Play requires a public page like this and a matching link in the Data safety form.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Muster and tap the settings icon.
  2. Tap Delete my account.
  3. Type DELETE and confirm.

This removes your name, matric number, PIN hash and all your attendance records from our servers, and clears your phone.

Lecturers

Open Settings, tap Delete my account, type DELETE and confirm. This removes your account, courses, class lists and past classes from our servers.

If you no longer have the app

Email efobifrancis53@gmail.com from your university email address with the subject "Delete my Muster account". Include your matric number and university. We will delete your data within 30 days and confirm by email.

What we may keep

Attendance lists a lecturer has already downloaded stay with that lecturer. Backups are overwritten within 30 days. Where the university must keep attendance records by law or regulation, we keep only those records, for as long as required.
