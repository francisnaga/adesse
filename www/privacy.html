Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 16, 2026

Summary: Muster is an educational attendance tracking platform. We do not sell your personal information, we do not show advertisements, and we do not track your location.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum information necessary to authenticate users and verify classroom attendance:

  • Student Accounts: Full name, student matriculation number, university, department, study level, and an encrypted hash of your attendance restore PIN.
  • Lecturer Accounts: Full name, institutional email address, university affiliation, and cryptographic public key parameters used for generating signed classroom session QR codes.
  • Google & Apple Sign-In Data: When you choose to sign in with Google or Apple, we receive basic authentication details: your verified email address, full name, and your unique authentication user ID. We use this strictly to link and protect your attendance records.
  • Classroom Attendance Logs: When a student scans a rotating QR code during class, the session timestamp, course identifier, and verification signature are logged.
  • Camera Permissions: The device camera is accessed solely on-demand to scan the classroom attendance QR code. Video frames and photos are processed locally on your device in real-time and are never stored, saved, or transmitted to any server.

2. How We Use Collected Information

Your information is used solely to:

  • Record and calculate course attendance percentages.
  • Provide lecturers with accurate class attendance registers and review queues.
  • Alert students when attendance falls below institutional minimum thresholds.
  • Enable account recovery and data restoration when switching devices.

3. Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance

Muster's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use Google user data for advertising, market research, or training artificial intelligence models.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal data to third parties. Data is stored securely in our enterprise backend infrastructure powered by Supabase (PostgreSQL with Row Level Security) and Vercel hosting. Information is only accessible to authorized university instructors for their specific courses.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Attendance records are retained for the duration of the relevant academic session. Users may review, export, or permanently delete their accounts and associated records at any time directly through the in-app Settings menu or by visiting our Account Deletion Page.

6. Security

All communication is encrypted via HTTPS / TLS 1.3. Cryptographic attendance verification keys are generated locally on device hardware using the Web Crypto API, and student PINs are cryptographically salted and hashed before transmission.
