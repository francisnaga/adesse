Adesse records who attended a class. It is run by [ORGANISATION / NAME] ("we"), for [UNIVERSITY / DEPARTMENT]. Contact: [SUPPORT EMAIL].

What we collect

  • Students: full name, matric number, department, level, university, a random device identifier, a hashed 6-digit PIN (we never store the PIN itself), and the time and course of each class you scan into.
  • Lecturers: name, email, university, a public signing key, your private signing key (locked on your phone with your passphrase before it is uploaded, so we cannot read it), the courses you add, any class lists you paste in, and the classes you run.
  • We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or biometrics. The camera is used only to read the class QR code on the lecturer's screen. Images are not saved or sent anywhere.

Why we collect it

To record attendance, show you your own attendance percentage, show lecturers who attended, warn you when you are below your minimum, and let you restore your account on a new phone. We do not sell data and we do not show ads.

Who can see it

  • You see your own records.
  • The lecturer of a course sees the name, matric number and attendance of students in that course. Lecturers may paste class lists (matric numbers and names) into the app; those are stored with their account.
  • Our hosting provider, [Google Firebase / other], stores the data on our behalf.

How long we keep it

Attendance records are kept for [e.g. the academic session plus 6 months], then deleted unless the university is required to keep them. Your data on your phone stays until you clear it.

Your rights

You can see, correct or delete your data. To delete it yourself, open Settings in the app and choose Delete my account, or follow the steps on the delete account page. You may also email us. You can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Security

Data is sent over HTTPS. PINs are hashed before they leave your phone. No system is perfectly secure, so please choose a PIN you do not use elsewhere.

Children

Adesse is for university students and staff.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date above.
